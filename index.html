Questions, Answered

How long does delivery take?

Dispatched in 2 days. Metro cities in 4–6 days, and other pin codes in 6–8 days.

How do I care for my idol?

Dust with a soft dry cloth. A mild brass polish a few times a year is enough.

What if my idol arrives damaged?

Write to us within 48 hours with a photo of the packing and the idol. We replace it or refund the order.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Share the deity, material, and height, and we confirm the making time before you pay.
